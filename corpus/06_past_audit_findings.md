## 1. Findings

### 1.1 AF-2025-07 Structuring alerts closed at Level 1
Observation: 14 of 52 STRUCTURING alerts sampled in Q2 FY2025-26 were closed as false positives at Level 1 without Principal Officer review, contrary to SOP-AML section 2.2. Risk rating: High. Root cause: alert queue SLA pressure and no system block on Level 1 closure. Management action: system control to block Level 1 closure of STRUCTURING alerts; retrospective review of closed alerts. Status: Closed after verification.

### 1.2 AF-2025-11 Income variance overrides without documentation
Observation: 9 disbursals to new-to-credit borrowers had an income corroboration ratio below 0.40 and were approved by override without Head of Credit approval, contrary to Credit Policy section 2.3. Two of these accounts reached DPD above 60 within four months. Risk rating: High. Management action: hard stop in the loan origination system for new-to-credit ratio below 0.40. Status: Open, target date 31 Dec 2026.

### 1.3 AF-2026-02 Periodic KYC updation overdue for high-risk customers
Observation: 6 percent of high-risk customers had KYC updation overdue beyond the two-year periodicity. Risk rating: Medium. Management action: automated reminders and restriction on fresh sanctions until updation. Status: Open.

### 1.4 AF-2026-04 Early delinquency concentration in one sourcing partner
Observation: One sourcing partner accounted for 41 percent of accounts with DPD above 60 within six months of disbursal while contributing 12 percent of disbursals. Partner was not placed on watch as required by Credit Policy section 3.3. Risk rating: High. Status: Open.
