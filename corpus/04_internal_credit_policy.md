## 1. Scope

### 1.1 Products covered
This policy governs unsecured business loans, business lines of credit, merchant cash advances and supply chain finance extended to MSMEs through direct digital journeys and sourcing partners.

## 2. Income assessment

### 2.1 Corroboration of declared income
Declared monthly business income must be corroborated using bank statement analysis covering at least the last six months. The corroboration ratio is the average monthly bank statement credit divided by the declared monthly income, excluding loan disbursals and inter-account transfers.

### 2.2 Income variance thresholds
Where the corroboration ratio is below 0.60 (bank credits more than 40 percent below declared income), the application must be referred to a Credit Manager with documented justification. Where the ratio is below 0.40, the application must be declined or referred to the Fraud Control Unit; any override requires approval from the Head of Credit and must be recorded in the loan management system.

### 2.3 New-to-credit borrowers
For new-to-credit (first-time) borrowers without a bureau score, the maximum sanctioned amount is Rs 5,00,000 and the income corroboration ratio must be at least 0.70. No overrides are permitted for first-time borrowers below a ratio of 0.40.

## 3. Portfolio monitoring

### 3.1 Early delinquency trigger
Any account that reaches DPD above 60 within six months of disbursal is an Early Delinquency Trigger and an Early Warning Signal under the fraud risk framework. The Fraud Control Unit must complete a review within 15 days to determine whether the account should be classified as a Red Flagged Account.

### 3.2 Asset classification
Accounts are classified daily as Standard (DPD 0), SMA-0 (1-30), SMA-1 (31-60), SMA-2 (61-90) and NPA (above 90), in line with the Reserve Bank's prudential norms.

### 3.3 Sourcing partner concentration
No single sourcing partner may account for more than 20 percent of monthly disbursals. A partner whose sourced accounts show an early-delinquency rate more than twice the portfolio average must be placed on watch and its sourcing suspended pending a Risk Committee review.

### 3.4 Exposure limits
Exposure to a single borrower must not exceed Rs 50,00,000 across products. Aggregate exposure to any one product must be reported monthly to the Risk Management Committee along with its gross NPA ratio.
