## 1. Templates

### 1.1 Case note template
A case note contains: (1) Case reference and alert IDs; (2) Summary of the signal; (3) Facts established, each with its data source; (4) Customer and KYC profile review; (5) Applicable regulatory and policy obligations with clause references; (6) Assessment and rationale; (7) Recommended actions with owners and statutory deadlines; (8) Open questions and evidence gaps; (9) Reviewer sign-off.

### 1.2 Exception report template
An exception report contains: (1) Exception description; (2) Policy or regulatory requirement not met, with clause reference; (3) Population affected with counts and amounts; (4) Root cause hypothesis; (5) Risk rating (High, Medium, Low) with rationale; (6) Remediation actions, owner and due date; (7) Reviewer sign-off.

### 1.3 STR draft extract template
An STR draft extract prepared for the Principal Officer contains: (1) Reporting entity and branch/channel; (2) Accounts and customers involved; (3) Summary of suspicious transactions with dates, amounts and channels; (4) Grounds of suspicion written as a factual narrative explaining why the activity is inconsistent with the customer's profile; (5) Details of investigation and KYC review; (6) Action taken. The draft is for internal review only and must not be shared with the customer.
