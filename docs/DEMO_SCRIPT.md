# Kavach: Demo Script (under 3 minutes)

Rehearse this exact order. The four questions are one click each (the demo cards in the composer), and the data is planted so the results are deterministic.

**Before you start**
* Snowflake: `CALL KAVACH.CORE.REBASE_DATES();` so "this week" and "last 30 days" line up with today.
* Optional live feed: `ALTER TASK KAVACH.CORE.SIMULATE_ALERTS_TASK RESUME;` (suspend it afterwards).
* Persona (top right): **Priya Menon · FCU Analyst**. Theme: **Midnight**.

| # | Time | Click / say | What the judges see | Criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0:00 | Open the app | Preloader, then the hero with live KPIs (open alerts, exposure, GNPA). "Kavach turns a plain-English question into a cited, audit-ready finding." | Relevance |
| 1 | 0:15 | **01 · Early delinquency** | The pipeline streams: *Route → Cortex Analyst → Audit*. The answer shows the accounts at DPD > 60 within 6 months and total exposure. In the evidence panel, point at **Verified query** and the **generated SQL**. "Two people asking this differently get the same number: it resolves to one governed definition." | Technical |
| 2 | 0:45 | **02 · Structuring + AML policy** | Route **BOTH**. 3 accounts with sub-₹50k cash and wallet credits across cities. Click a **[P1]** chip: the citation card flashes, showing the SOP-AML structuring rule and the RBI KYC clause, with section numbers. "Every sentence is cited, and a citation that doesn't exist would turn the badge red." | Completeness (signal → evidence) |
| 3 | 1:20 | Select **Case note**, then **⚡ Generate finding** | A document slides in: finding ID, audit reference, confidence, narrative, and the SQL, rows and quoted clauses in the appendix. "The header and evidence appendix are generated by code. Only the narrative is the model, and it is constrained to the evidence." Click **Send for sign-off**. | Completeness (evidence → finding) |
| 4 | 1:50 | In Findings, switch persona to **Arjun Rao · Principal Officer**, pick **Approve**, then **Record sign-off** | Status becomes Approved and the sign-off is its own audit event. "Maker-checker is recorded as data: self-reviews are flagged." | Governance |
| 5 | 2:10 | Back in Copilot, **04 · SBR governance** | Route **POLICY**, no SQL. Clause-level citations: Middle Layer threshold, CCO, compliance function, disclosures. "It's not only good at SQL." | Relevance |
| 6 | 2:35 | Open **Audit** | The integrity ring is green (N of N records verify). Click the newest row to see the full JSON payload: SQL, clauses, answer, model. "If a regulator asks how we decided this, this is the replay. Change one byte and the ring turns red." | Technical |

**Bonus (if time allows):** in **Alerts**, click **Investigate** on the HIGH `INCOME_MISMATCH` alert. Kavach finds **6** mismatched first-time borrowers, **2 of which have no rule alert**. That's the copilot catching what the rules engine missed.

**Fallback:** if Cortex is slow or unavailable, run the API with `KAVACH_BACKEND=mock`. The same flow works offline, with deterministic answers, the same SQL from the semantic model, and a real hash chain.
